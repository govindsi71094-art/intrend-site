# InTrend

Home page, privacy policy and terms of service for **InTrend**, a YouTube channel that publishes two short
NIFTY 50 market-analysis videos every NSE trading day. The InTrend app publishes and manages that
channel through the YouTube Data and Analytics APIs.

- Home: https://govindsi71094-art.github.io/intrend-site/
- Privacy Policy: https://govindsi71094-art.github.io/intrend-site/privacy.html
- Terms of Service: https://govindsi71094-art.github.io/intrend-site/terms.html

Questions or data-deletion requests: open an issue in this repository.
