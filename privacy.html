Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains what the InTrend app (“the App”) does with data, including data received from Google APIs. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the InTrend YouTube channel to publish and manage that channel. It is not offered to the public, and it does not sign in any other user.

Google user data the App accesses

When the channel owner authorises the App, it receives the following permissions for the InTrend channel only:

Permission (scope)What it is used for
youtube.uploadUploading InTrend's daily videos.
youtubeSetting thumbnails, adding videos to InTrend playlists, and reading the channel's own ID.
youtube.force-sslPosting one poll comment on each InTrend video, and reading the public comments on InTrend's own videos to count viewer votes.
yt-analytics.readonlyReading the performance of InTrend's own videos, such as views, watch time, average view duration and subscriber changes, to report it to the channel owner.

Comments from viewers

InTrend videos invite viewers to comment BULL, BEAR or SIDEWAYS. The App reads these public comments through the YouTube API. It uses each commenter's public channel ID only while counting, so that each person is counted once. It then stores only the totals, for example “21 bull, 9 bear, 5 sideways”. Comment text and commenter IDs are not stored, published or shared.

How data is used and shared

  • Data is used only to publish InTrend videos and report their performance to the channel owner.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

The App's access token and the aggregated statistics (vote totals and video performance numbers) are stored on the channel owner's private server. Access to that server is restricted. No other user data is kept.

Retention and deletion

Aggregated statistics are kept while the channel operates, and deleted on request. Access can be revoked at any time in Google account permissions. After revocation the App can no longer read or change anything. Viewers can delete their own YouTube comments at any time.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. Use of YouTube is governed by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated, and the date at the top will change when it is. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at https://github.com/govindsi71094-art/intrend-site/issues.
