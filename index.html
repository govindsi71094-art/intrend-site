Daily NIFTY 50 market analysis, in two short videos

InTrend is a YouTube channel about the Indian stock market. Every NSE trading day it publishes a pre-market outlook and a market pulse covering the data, the news and the odds, plus a viewer poll.

Pre-Market Outlook · 08:00 IST

Global cues, NIFTY's chart and key levels, India VIX and the expected range, breadth of the 50 stocks, the odds of a gap-up, and what to confirm after 9:15.

Market Pulse · 08:40 IST

The top stories, the data dashboard (futures, option chain, FII/DII positioning, currency and crude), "bullish or bearish?" story polls, and the overall trend meter.

How the numbers are made

Probabilities shown in the videos come from statistical models that were tested on years of market data they never saw while being built. Inputs that did not improve those tests are shown as context only. Every outlook is logged and graded against what the market actually did, and the result is shown in the next video.

About the InTrend app

The channel is run by a private automation tool used only by the channel owner. It uploads InTrend's videos, sets thumbnails and playlists, posts one poll comment on each video, counts viewer votes from public comments, and reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics. It does not access any other person's YouTube or Google account. See the Privacy Policy for details.

InTrend videos are market analysis for education. They are not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security, and probabilities describe past patterns that can be wrong.
